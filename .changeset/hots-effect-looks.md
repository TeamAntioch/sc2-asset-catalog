Add `@team-antioch/sc2-hots-effects`: `data/hots-effect-looks.json` says what each of Heroes of the Storm's default-skin effect models looks like, written by agents from frames of it filmed in StarCraft II on a Marine, keyed by the model's path, in the same shape as the catalog's effect looks. `readHotsEffectLooks` and `parseHotsEffectLooks` read it with the catalog's effect look schema.
