Add the Set Finder, `@team-antioch/sc2-set-finder`: ask for a place to film a cutscene in and see Blizzard's campaign cutscene sets that fit, narrowed by keyword and the kit's embeddings over the set looks and ranked by Jev, on a local page with each set's pictures linked from the sc2-mapping-reference gallery. `npx @team-antioch/sc2-set-finder --open` starts it; `startSetFinder`, `openSetFinder`, `setCards` and `setAnswer` let a host lay its own sets over the published ones.
